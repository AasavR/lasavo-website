Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 | Lasavo LLC (New Mexico, USA)

1. Information We Collect

Lasavo LLC operates a decentralized, non-custodial savings yield pool protocol. We collect only the minimum required information to provide passwordless single sign-on authentication and transaction routing: email address for OTP delivery, public blockchain wallet addresses, and public on-chain transaction data.

2. How Information is Used

Your email address is strictly used to transmit one-time sign-in passwords (OTP) and route on-chain smart vault balances. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third-party advertisers.

3. Blockchain Transactions & Public Data

Transactions executed on Monad Mainnet and Coinbase Base are recorded on public decentralized ledgers and are publicly visible by their nature.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at help@lasavo.org.
