Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What is the absolute minimum amount I can invest?
There is zero minimum. You can deposit 0.0001 ETH (~$0.25) or any micro fraction. Lasavo has zero barrier to entry.
Do I need to install a browser extension to try?
No! You can immediately use our built-in instant burner wallet with demo testnet credits, or connect your existing self-custody wallet anytime.
Can I withdraw my deposit at any time?
Yes, 100%. Lasavo has zero lock-up periods and zero withdrawal penalties. You can exit partially or completely at any moment.
Do you accept fiat or bank transfers?
No. Lasavo serves exclusively customers who are already on the blockchain. We do not accept fiat currencies or bank transfers.
