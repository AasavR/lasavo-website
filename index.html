Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is there a minimum deposit amount?
No. Lasavo is designed so anyone, even micro-investors with fractions of a dollar (e.g., 0.0001 ETH), can participate and experience compounding yield without gating barriers.
Can I withdraw my deposit at any time?
Yes, 100%. Lasavo has zero lock-up periods and zero withdrawal penalties. You can exit partially or completely at any moment.
How does the Testnet to Mainnet transition work?
Our contracts are currently deployed on Base Sepolia and Monad Testnet for public verification. Once smart contract testing is completed, identical audited contracts deploy directly to Base Mainnet (Chain ID 8453) and Monad Mainnet.
Do you accept fiat or bank transfers?
No. Lasavo serves exclusively customers who are already on the blockchain. We do not accept fiat currencies or bank transfers.
