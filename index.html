FAQs

Common questions

Who can use Lasavo?
+
Lasavo is built exclusively for users who already hold crypto on-chain. We onboard blockchain natives only — no fiat deposits, no bank accounts, no KYC. If you have a self-custody wallet, you are in.
Who holds my funds?
+
You do. Lasavo is fully non-custodial. Your assets are held in smart contracts on Monad and Coinbase Base. Only you can authorize withdrawals.
Can I withdraw at any time?
+
Yes. There are no lock-ups, no vesting, no waiting periods. Withdraw your principal and yield in full at any time. The stablecoin reserve ensures immediate liquidity.
What is the fee structure?
+
Lasavo charges 10% on the yield it generates — and nothing else. No deposit fee, no withdrawal fee, no management fee. If Lasavo does not earn yield, it does not earn a fee.
What wallets are supported?
+
Any wallet supporting WalletConnect or compatible with Monad or Coinbase Base. MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, Rainbow, Ledger, Trezor, and more.
What are the risks?
+
All DeFi carries smart contract risk. Lasavo contracts are audited. Pool strategies carry market risk — returns are not guaranteed. Never deposit more than you can afford to lose.
Is there a Lasavo token?
+
No. Lasavo does not have a governance or project token. We focus entirely on delivering yield to our users.
What chains will Lasavo support in future?
+
We are evaluating additional EVM-compatible networks. Current focus is Monad and Coinbase Base. Follow us on X for announcements.
