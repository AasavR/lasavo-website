Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

How does sending USDC to an email ID work?
Just like Rift, Lasavo links each recipient's email address to an on-chain smart wallet via cryptographic account abstraction. When you type their email ID, the USDC is routed to their account. If they haven't signed up yet, the funds wait securely until they log in with their email OTP.
Is there any password required to log in?
No passwords ever. You simply enter your email ID, receive a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP), and are instantly logged into your profile with full wallet access.
What currency does Lasavo use?
Lasavo is 100% USDC-denominated. You deposit USDC, hold USDC, send USDC to emails, and earn compounded yield in USDC at 14.8% APY.
Can I withdraw my USDC at any time?
Yes, 100%. Lasavo has zero lock-up periods and zero withdrawal penalties. You can withdraw your principal and accrued USDC yield instantly.
